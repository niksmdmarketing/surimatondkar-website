About

Suri Matondkar listens to Taylor Swift and overthinks / writes things.

She writes literary fiction and narrative non-fiction, and is completing a Doctor of Creative Arts at Western Sydney University as an RTP scholar. Her non-fiction won the 2025 Woollahra Digital Literary Award and the 2024 Lord Mayor’s Creative Writing Awards, and in 2025 she was an Eric Dark Flagship Fellow at Varuna, The National Writers’ House.
